BMI Formula

Metric Formula

BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ Height (m)²

Example: Weight 70 kg, Height 1.75 m: 70 ÷ (1.75)² = 22.86

Imperial Formula

BMI = (Weight (lbs) ÷ Height (in)²) × 703

Example: Weight 154 lbs, Height 69 inches: (154 ÷ 69²) × 703 = 22.75

BMI Categories

  • Underweight: BMI less than 18.5
  • Healthy Weight (Normal): BMI 18.5 to 24.9
  • Overweight: BMI 25 to 29.9
  • Obese (Class I): BMI 30 to 34.9
  • Obese (Class II): BMI 35 to 39.9
  • Severe Obesity (Class III): BMI 40 or higher

Example Calculation

Example

Weight: 70 kg | Height: 175 cm

Calculation: 70 ÷ (1.75)² = 70 ÷ 3.0625 = 22.86

Result: BMI of 22.86 falls in the "Healthy Weight" category (18.5 - 24.9)

Important Information About BMI

Limitations of BMI

  • Muscle mass: BMI doesn't distinguish between muscle and fat. Athletes may have a high BMI despite being fit.
  • Age and gender: BMI doesn't account for age-related changes or gender differences in body composition.
  • Bone density: People with denser bones may have a higher BMI without excess body fat.
  • Children: BMI for children should be interpreted differently than for adults.
  • Overall health: BMI is just one indicator. Overall fitness, strength, and health metrics are also important.

When to See a Doctor

If your BMI calculation shows you're underweight or obese, or if you have concerns about your weight and health, consult with a healthcare professional who can provide personalized advice based on your complete health profile.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is BMI accurate for everyone? ▼

BMI is a general screening tool, but it has limitations. It doesn't account for muscle mass, bone density, or age. Athletes and very muscular individuals may have a high BMI despite being healthy. Always consult a healthcare provider for personalized assessment.

What's the difference between my height in feet/inches and centimeters? ▼

These are just different measurement systems. 1 inch = 2.54 cm. The calculator allows you to use either system - simply select the unit type that's most convenient for you.

Can children use this BMI calculator? ▼

This calculator is designed for adults. Children and teenagers should use age-specific BMI charts, as their body composition changes as they grow. Consult a pediatrician for children's BMI assessment.

What should I do if my BMI is outside the healthy range? ▼

Schedule an appointment with your doctor. They can assess your individual health situation, consider your overall fitness level, medical history, and provide personalized recommendations for diet, exercise, and lifestyle changes if needed.

Is my data stored anywhere? ▼

No. All calculations happen in your browser. Your weight, height, and any health information you enter is never sent to any server or stored anywhere. Your privacy is completely protected.
