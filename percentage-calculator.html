How to Calculate Percentages

Basic Percentage Formula

The basic formula for calculating a percentage is:

(Part ÷ Whole) × 100 = Percentage

Common Percentage Calculations

  • Calculate a percentage of a number: (Percentage ÷ 100) × Number
  • Find what percentage one number is of another: (Part ÷ Whole) × 100
  • Calculate percentage increase: ((New Value - Old Value) ÷ Old Value) × 100
  • Calculate percentage decrease: ((Old Value - New Value) ÷ Old Value) × 100

Real-World Examples

  • Sales discount: A shirt costs $50. It's on sale for 30% off. How much do you save? 50 × 0.30 = $15 saved. New price: $35
  • Tip calculation: Your bill is $80. You want to tip 20%. Tip amount: 80 × 0.20 = $16
  • Grade calculation: You got 45 points out of 50. What's your percentage? (45 ÷ 50) × 100 = 90%
  • Growth rate: Revenue was $10,000 last year, $12,000 this year. Growth: ((12,000 - 10,000) ÷ 10,000) × 100 = 20%

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate 15% off a price? ▼

Multiply the original price by 0.15 to find the discount amount, then subtract from the original price. For example: $100 × 0.15 = $15 discount. Final price: $100 - $15 = $85.

What's the difference between percentage points and percent? ▼

A percentage point is an absolute difference. If something goes from 10% to 15%, that's a 5 percentage point increase. A percent (or relative change) would be: (15-10)÷10 × 100 = 50% increase.

How do I calculate a percentage increase? ▼

Use this formula: ((New Value - Old Value) ÷ Old Value) × 100. Example: If a price goes from $50 to $75: ((75-50)÷50) × 100 = 50% increase.

Can I calculate percentages with decimals? ▼

Yes! Our calculator supports decimal numbers. You can calculate percentages with values like 25.5, 33.33, etc.

Is there a quick way to calculate 10% of something? ▼

Yes! To find 10% of any number, just move the decimal point one place to the left. For example, 10% of 250 = 25. To find 20%, multiply that result by 2.
