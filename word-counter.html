How Word Counting Works

What Each Metric Means

  • Words: Individual words separated by spaces or punctuation. Contractions like "don't" count as one word.
  • Characters: Every letter, number, and symbol including spaces.
  • Characters (no spaces): Total characters excluding whitespace.
  • Sentences: Segments ending with periods, question marks, or exclamation points.
  • Paragraphs: Blocks of text separated by line breaks.
  • Reading Time: Estimated time to read the text at average reading speed (200 words per minute).

Typical Word Counts

  • Tweet: 30-50 words
  • Short paragraph: 100-200 words
  • Blog post: 800-2000 words
  • Article: 1500-3000 words
  • Short story: 5000-20000 words
  • Novel: 70000-100000+ words

What Counts as a Word?

Our word counter uses the standard definition of a word: any sequence of characters separated by spaces or punctuation. This means:

  • Contractions (don't, it's, we'll) = 1 word each
  • Hyphenated words (well-known, mother-in-law) = typically 1 word
  • Numbers (123, 2023) = 1 word each
  • Abbreviations (Mr., Dr., etc.) = 1 word each

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my word count different from another tool? ▼

Different word counters may handle special cases differently (like hyphenated words, contractions, or numbers). Our counter uses the standard definition: words are separated by spaces or punctuation. Some tools may count differently, but the differences are usually small.

How is reading time calculated? ▼

Reading time is based on an average reading speed of 200 words per minute. This is the typical speed for adults reading online content at a comfortable pace. Complex or technical material may take longer to read.

Does punctuation count as characters? ▼

Yes. Commas, periods, apostrophes, and all other punctuation marks count as characters. Spaces also count as characters in the "Characters" count but not in "Characters (no spaces)".

Can I use this for academic work? ▼

Yes! Our word counter is perfect for essays, assignments, and academic papers. Just paste your text and check if you've met word count requirements. However, always verify the count with your institution's standard if they have specific requirements.

Is my text stored or analyzed? ▼

No! All text analysis happens in your browser. Your text is never sent to any server or stored anywhere. Your privacy is completely protected.

Can I count characters in a document? ▼

Yes! Copy the text from your document and paste it into our word counter. It will instantly show you all the statistics including word count, character count, and reading time.
