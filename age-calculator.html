How the Age Calculator Works

What It Calculates

The age calculator computes your exact age by finding the difference between your date of birth and today's date. It breaks down the result into:

  • Years: Complete years from birth
  • Months: Additional complete months beyond the years
  • Days: Additional days beyond the months
  • Total Days: The total number of days you've lived
  • Days Until Birthday: How many days until your next birthday

How It's Calculated

The calculator takes your birth date and compares it to today's date (or any date you choose). It then calculates:

  1. The number of complete years
  2. The remaining months after accounting for years
  3. The remaining days after accounting for months
  4. The total number of days lived
  5. The days remaining until the next birthday

Leap Years

The calculator accounts for leap years automatically. A leap year occurs every 4 years (except for years divisible by 100, which are not leap years unless also divisible by 400). This means February has 29 days instead of 28 in leap years.

Example

Example Calculation

Date of Birth: January 15, 2000

Current Date: September 28, 2026

Result:

  • Age: 26 years, 8 months, and 13 days
  • Total Days: 9,766 days
  • Next Birthday: January 15, 2027 (109 days away)

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does the calculator show months and days separately? ▼

This provides your exact age. Someone might be "26 years and 8 months" old, which is more precise than just saying "26 years." Months vary in length (28-31 days), so the calculator accounts for this accurately.

How are leap years handled? ▼

If your birthday is February 29 (leap day), the calculator will recognize this and calculate your age correctly. For non-leap years, your birthday is considered to be March 1 for age calculation purposes.

Can I calculate the age of someone else? ▼

Yes! Simply enter any date of birth and the calculator will show the age of that person or event as of today's date.

What's the difference between age in days and age in years? ▼

Age in years is how we typically express age (e.g., "I'm 26 years old"). Age in days is the total number of days you've lived since birth, which is more precise for scientific or medical purposes.

Is my birthdate stored anywhere? ▼

No! All calculations happen in your browser. Your birthdate is never sent to any server or stored anywhere. Your privacy is completely protected.
